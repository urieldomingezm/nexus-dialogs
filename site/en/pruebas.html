Testing bench

A bench to check the library with your own eyes: choose how it looks, open the dialogs, go through the checklist and, if something fails or if everything goes well, tell us with one click. Nothing to install.

1. Choose the appearance

It simulates a page with Tailwind (or Bootstrap, Material) and its light or dark mode. It uses followPageScheme(): when the mode changes, the dialogs you open afterwards come out in the new mode.

Color theme

Page mode

Bootstrap and Material only have a light palette: in dark mode Nexus’s is used.

2. Open the dialogs

Each button opens a real case. Try them with the mouse and the keyboard (Tab, Esc, Enter), and repeat with another theme or mode.

3. What to check

Tick what you tested: it is saved in this browser. What you can’t test (for example a screen reader), leave unticked.

    4. Tell us

    This summary includes the version, your browser, the chosen appearance and your checklist. Copy it or open it directly in a report.
